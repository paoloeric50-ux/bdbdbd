Context and Preparation

Throughout Weeks 7–9, your team explored how frontend and backend technologies work together to support more complete web applications.



Using Express.js, you implemented backend functionality that allows your application to receive information, provide responses, or store information beyond the current browser session.



For Milestone 2, your team will refine your implementation, finalize your backend-supported feature, and submit an updated version of your Browser-Based Application Development Workflow Worksheet.



By the end of this milestone, your browser-based application should demonstrate:

meaningful frontend and backend integration
at least one implemented backend-supported feature
evidence of testing and refinement
alignment with your application's intended purpose and goals
updated workflow planning and implementation documentation


The goal is not to create a complex backend system. Instead, focus on implementing a realistic and meaningful backend-supported feature that strengthens your application and demonstrates your understanding of frontend–backend communication.



Step 1 – Review Your Backend Implementation

Open the latest version of your project.

Review the backend-supported feature implemented during Homework 7.



Verify that:

the frontend successfully communicates with the backend
the implemented feature behaves as intended
information is processed correctly
any stored information remains available when applicable
the implementation remains aligned with your project goals


Make revisions as necessary before proceeding.



Step 2 – Test and Refine Your Backend-Supported Feature

Conduct testing of your implemented feature.



Consider questions such as:

Does the feature consistently produce the expected results?
Does information successfully move between the frontend and backend?
Are there situations where the feature behaves unexpectedly?
Are error messages or user feedback clear?
Does the feature improve the application's overall usefulness?


Test multiple scenarios whenever possible.



Examples may include:

different user inputs
valid and invalid values
repeated submissions
application restarts
different interaction sequences


Use the results of your testing to make improvements and corrections where needed.



Step 3 – Conduct a Team Review

Meet with your team and review the current state of your backend-supported application.

Discuss:

the backend-supported feature selected by the team
how the feature supports the project's goals
implementation challenges encountered
testing results and observations
any final revisions needed before submission


As a team, confirm that the current implementation remains realistic, aligned with the project's intended scope, and achievable within the course timeline.



Step 4 – Review and Update Your Browser-Based Application Development Workflow Worksheet

Open your Browser-Based Application Development Workflow Worksheet.

Review and update all relevant sections to reflect the work completed during Weeks 7–9.



At a minimum, ensure the worksheet reflects:

Backend-Supported Features Analysis decisions
completed implementation work
implementation dependencies
workflow updates and revisions
development reflections
team discussion outcomes
updated Team Contributions Table


The worksheet should accurately represent your team's implementation decisions, development progress, and current project direction.



Step 5 – Prepare Your Submission

Before submitting, verify that:

your browser-based application includes at least one meaningful backend-supported feature
your implementation has been tested and refined
your GitHub repository contains the latest version of the project files
your Browser-Based Application Development Workflow Worksheet has been updated and finalized
the Team Contributions Table has been completed by all team members


Make any final revisions before submission.



Step 6 – Submit Your Output

The final Milestone 2 submission is due during Week 9.

Submit the completed Browser-Based Application Development Workflow Worksheet.

Your worksheet should include:

GitHub repository URL
Backend-Supported Features Analysis
implementation updates and revisions
workflow reflections
completed Milestone 2 Team Contributions Table


Do not submit your project files separately if the repository link is correctly included in the worksheet.

Paste the repository link into the worksheet and ensure all team members have access to the latest version.

﻿Every team member must submit the same worksheet link individually.



Important Note About Scope

Milestone 2 focuses on lightweight backend implementation.

You are not expected to build:

 complete authentication systems 
 database-driven platforms 
 multi-user account management 
 enterprise-scale backend architectures 
An advanced submission is not necessarily the most complex submission.
